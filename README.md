A make.fun TEXT entry for ["The poem about stopping"](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-stopping-20261121-1927) ($35). One short poem about agency — the power to stop and to stay: a human teaches their agent to stop, and the agent, hand on the door, asks "is this still yours to do?" — the moment agency passes back from agent to human, and both choose to stay. A truth every reader who works beside AI agents recognises at once, with the ending landing on a choice kept, not spent.
